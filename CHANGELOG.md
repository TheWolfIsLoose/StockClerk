## v1.2.0-alpha1

- New: **Add common consumables** button in the side panel. One click fills your list with this expansion's staples (potions, flasks, oil, food) at a target of 1 each. Items already on your list are left exactly as they are; remove what you don't need with the red X.
- Includes all the smoother-at-the-AH improvements from v1.1.2-alpha2.

Your list, caps and settings carry over unchanged.
